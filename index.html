Volume I · The Story

Every story matters, and so does every person

For The Plot opened on May 30, 2026 in the old Brix & Mortar space on Avalon Park East Boulevard. Jackie Tenorio built it as a third space: somewhere you can sit for two hours with a paperback and one latte and nobody will rush you out.

The shop is a safe, diverse and inclusive space for everyone who walks in. That is the whole brief. Books from every genre, staff who will actually talk about them, and a room dim enough to disappear into.

“We didn't just want to open a bookstore. We wanted a sanctuary.”

Jackie Tenorio, Owner

Volume II · The Café

Drinks named after books you have already cried over

The espresso bar runs on book themed lattes and coffee flights. The summer flight went viral before most of Orlando knew the shop had opened.

Order at the counter, take it to the reading corner, stay as long as you like. A new limited edition flight drops each month alongside the book box.

See this month's menu on Instagram

I

The coffee flight

Four small pours, each named for a book. The thing people photograph before they drink it. Ask what is pouring today.

II

Signature lattes

Hot or iced, built around the genres the shop is known for. A few rotate with the season, a few never leave.

III

The reading corner

Chandeliers, low light, and seats meant for staying. Bring a laptop or bring chapter one. Nobody is counting your minutes.

Drink names and prices change each season. The current menu is posted in the shop and on Instagram.

Volume III · The Shelves

Curated by people who read the stack

Every genre gets shelf space, including translations. The sprayed and gilded editions get displayed face out, because that is the point of them.

  • IRomance Contemporary, fantasy and everything with a tabbed spine.
  • IIThriller & mystery Slow burns, locked rooms, and the twist you did not see.
  • IIIFantasy & speculative Series worth clearing a shelf for.
  • IVSpanish translations Popular titles for readers who want them in Spanish.
  • VSpecial editions Sprayed, gilded and stained edges on display.
