The Book Box

One title a month, chosen by the people who run the shelves, signed when the author says yes. Packed in store, picked up in store or shipped.

See the boxes Join the waitlist

What is inside

A book, and the things that go with it

Every box is built around a single title. The extras are chosen to match that book, not padded out with whatever was left in the stockroom.

  • IThe title A new or recent release, signed when the author is able.
  • IITwo to three extras Bookmarks, sleeves, candles, pins. Local makers where possible.
  • IIIA card from the shop Why this book, what to read next, what pairs with it.
  • IVA café pairing The drink built for that month's title, free on your next visit.

How it works

Four steps, then it just shows up

  1. I

    Tell us what you read

    Pick your genres and name a few books you loved. That is what the picks are matched against.

  2. II

    We choose the title

    One book a month, picked by staff. If the author can sign it, they do, and the box says so.

  3. III

    Boxes go out on release day

    Packed in the shop. Subscriber copies are held until closing if you are picking up.

  4. IV

    Come get your drink

    The pairing drink is on the house for the month. Bring the card, or just tell us your name.
