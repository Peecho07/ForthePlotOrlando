    The Regulars

    What runs here every month

    I

    Bookish Trivia Night

    Put your shelf-knowledge to the test. Rounds cover classic literature, tropes and the latest viral hits, and you play for bragging rights and shop prizes.

    II

    Book to Movie Screening

    Ever argued that the book was better? Now is your chance to prove it. Full length adaptations on the big screen, plus premiere nights for the first episodes of new book based series.

    III

    Book Club

    One title a month, discussed in the shop. Pick up the current book at the counter and read at whatever pace your week allows.

    IV

    Writer Workshops

    Working sessions for people who are actually writing. Bring pages and a drink order. Seats are limited, so sign up in store.

    V

    Author Signings

    Local and touring authors sign in store. If you are an author and want a date, fill out the interest form on the Authors page.

    VI

    Community Collab Table

    A local creator or artisan takes the front table for the day. If you make something and want to show it, the Vendors form gets the plot started.
