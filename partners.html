Authors, Publishers & Vendors

Three ways to end up in the shop: sign your book at the front table, get your list on the shelves, or take the community collab table for a day.

Authors

Book an in-store signing

If you are an author and want to sign in the shop, fill out the interest form. Local, touring, traditionally published or indie, all of it gets read.

  • ISignings run most weekends Usually early afternoon, in the front of the shop.
  • IIThe shop handles stock Tell us where to order from, or bring your own on consignment.
  • IIIWe promote it Instagram, TikTok, the in-store calendar and the mailing list.
  • IVSigned stock stays Leftover signed copies go on the shelf with a signed sticker.

Author interest form

Publishers

Get your list in front of readers

The shop buys across romance, thriller, fantasy and translation, with a standing interest in diverse voices and Spanish language editions.

  • IGalleys and ARCs Send them. Staff picks and shelf talkers come out of what gets read.
  • IIBook box placement One title a month goes in the box. Signed copies get priority.
  • IIIAuthor tours Central Florida stops, with promotion through the shop's channels.
  • IVSpecial editions Sprayed, gilded and stained edge runs sell well here and get displayed face out.

Publisher submission

Vendors

Take the community collab table

Are you a local creator or artisan looking to showcase your work? The front table is yours for a day. Fill out the form to get the plot started.

  • IOne maker at a time You get the table, the sign and the foot traffic for the day.
  • IIBookish work fits best Candles, art, jewelry, stationery, anything readers reach for.
  • IIIYou keep your sales Bring your own payment setup. The shop takes nothing off the table.
  • IVWholesale is separate If you want to stock the shelf year round, say so on the form.

Vendor interest form

Response time

Every form goes to the same inbox

Someone reads it. If it is a fit, you will hear back with dates. If you would rather just talk to a person, the shop's contact details are one page over.
