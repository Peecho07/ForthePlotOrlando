Volume II · The Café

Coffee Shop

Drinks named after books you have already cried over. Order at the counter, take it to the reading corner, and stay as long as you like.

At the counter

The espresso bar runs on book themed lattes, each one titled like something off the shelves. The board in the shop is this same menu, printed as a library card.

Nothing here is a two hour minimum and nothing here is a two hour maximum. One latte buys you the chair for as long as the chapter takes.

    The Fine Print

    Ordering, sitting, staying

    I

    Order at the counter

    Walk in, order by title, and take it to the reading corner. Everything on this page is on the card by the register.

    II

    Make it yours

    Coconut, oat and almond milk, twenty odd flavors, cold foam on anything iced. Sugar free syrups are on the list too.

    III

    Stay a while

    Chandeliers, low light, and seats meant for staying. Bring a laptop or bring chapter one. Nobody is counting your minutes.
